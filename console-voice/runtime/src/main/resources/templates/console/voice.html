Voice

Talk with the agent out loud. Speak, hear the reply, and interrupt whenever you like. It is the same conversation as the chat.

Idle

Start the microphone to talk with the agent.

--:-- left
Mode
Push-to-talk: hold Space or this button while you speak. Escape interrupts the reply.

Speech output: buffered, one sentence at a time.

Use headphones so the agent's voice is not picked up by the microphone and taken for your interruption.
