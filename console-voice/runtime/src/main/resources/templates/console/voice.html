Voice

Talk to the agent live. Your speech is sent in utterances and the replies are spoken back. The speech adapters are buffered: each sentence is synthesized whole before it plays, so there is a pause before each one. No real-time latency is promised.

Idle

Speech output: buffered, one sentence at a time.

Use headphones so the agent's voice is not picked up by the microphone and taken for your interruption.
